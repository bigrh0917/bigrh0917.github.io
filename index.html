About me

I’m Yuchen Li, an AI engineer working on large language models, from training and fine-tuning to LLM applications in production.

In my current and previous roles I’ve built Graph RAG and multi-agent systems, including a self-hosted Qwen3 deployment on vLLM and a Text-to-SQL analytics PoC for accounting data.

  • Built a Llama-style Transformer from scratch (GQA, RoPE, SwiGLU) and served it with FastAPI.
  • Fine-tuned Qwen2.5-7B with LoRA on 790K Chinese medical Q&A pairs on a single 24GB GPU.
  • Built a RAG chat backend with hybrid search and streaming responses.

Stack: PyTorch · Transformers · LoRA / Unsloth · vLLM · FastAPI · LangChain · Neo4j · Elasticsearch

Education: M.Eng. in Artificial Intelligence (2025) · B.Eng. in Civil Engineering (2022)
